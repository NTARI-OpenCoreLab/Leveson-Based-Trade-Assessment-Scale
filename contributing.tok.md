## ni li seme?

nimi "LBTAS" li tan nimi Inli "Leveson-Based Trade Assessment Scale". ona li ilo pi pana nanpa: ona li lukin e esun, li pana e nanpa pi esun ni. ilo ni li kepeken toki ilo "Python". lipu ilo suli ona li "lbtas.py".

jan ni li kepeken ona:

- jan pi pali sona — ona li kepeken nanpa LBTAS lon lipu sona ona
- jan pi pali ilo — ona li wan e LBTAS lon ilo ante
- jan pi kulupu NTARI — ona li toki e pakala, li toki e wile sin

## sina wile pali la

pali ale li kama lon ma toki "Slack" pi kulupu NTARI:
https://ntari.slack.com/archives/C09N88JN2SH

o toki e pali sina lon ma ni. o open e pali sina lon tenpo kama.

lipu ilo li lon "GitHub":

```bash
git clone https://github.com/NTARI-OpenCoreLab/Leveson-Based-Trade-Assessment-Scale.git
cd Leveson-Based-Trade-Assessment-Scale
git checkout -b feature/your-feature-name
```

nasin pali:

1. o toki lon "Slack" lon tenpo pini pi pali sina.
2. o pali lon linja sina ("branch").
3. o kepeken nasin sitelen "PEP 8". o pana e toki sona lon insa pi pali sina. o awen lili e pali wan.
4. o lukin e pali sina: ona li pali pona ala pona?
5. nimi pi pana pali ("commit") li kepeken nasin `type: brief description`, kepeken nimi `feat`, `fix`, `docs`, `refactor`, `test`, `chore`.
6. o open e "pull request" tawa linja "main". o toki e ijo sina pali, e tan ona.

## nasin pona pi kulupu

- o pona tawa jan ale.
- o toki e sona pi ilo, ala e ike jan.
- o kute e toki pi jan ante lon pali sina.
- o ala toki ike tan kulupu jan, tan selo jan, tan ijo ante pi jan.
- o ala pana e sona len pi jan ante.

jan li pakala e nasin ni la, ni li ken kama:

1. toki pi wile ante
2. pini lili pi pali kulupu
3. pini ale pi pali kulupu

o toki e pakala tawa forge@ntari.org

## lawa lipu

sina pana e pali la, pali sina li lon lawa "AGPL-3.0". pali sina o tan sina, anu o jo e nimi pi jan pali ona. ona o ala kama jo e ijo pi jan ante.
